# Privacy Policy

**YT Auto Miniplayer**

Last update: 3 September 2026

## The short answer

The extension collects no data. It sends no data. It has no server.

## What the extension does

The extension runs on `www.youtube.com` and `youtube.com` only. On a
watch page, it looks for a link click, a search, and the back button.
When one of them occurs, it sends the same `i` keyboard shortcut that
YouTube uses to open the miniplayer.

To do this, the extension reads the address of the current page and
parts of the page structure. It keeps none of this. It transmits none
of this.

## What the extension keeps

The extension keeps three settings on your device, in the local
extension storage of the browser:

- Automatic miniplayer: on or off.
- Also on the back button: on or off.
- The minimum number of seconds on the page.

You set these values in the toolbar popup. They stay on your device.
To remove them, remove the extension.

## What the extension does not do

- It makes no network request.
- It uses no analytics, no tracking, and no cookies.
- It reads no account data, no video data, and no page text.
- It sells and transfers no data, because it holds none.

## Permissions

| Permission                     | Why                                             |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `storage`                      | To keep the three settings on your device.      |
| Host access to `youtube.com`   | To open the YouTube miniplayer inside the page. |
